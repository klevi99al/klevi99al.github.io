worldspawn // about

I learned level design by making Zombies maps

Before games were my job, I was building custom Call of Duty Zombies maps in Radiant, scripting them in GSC and publishing them on UGX-Mods. One of them took about two years, ending in a full Easter egg quest and a boss fight.

That habit stuck. I like owning a mechanic from the first prototype to the moment a player gets it, whether that's a puzzle in Unreal or a merge game on a low-end phone.

Based in Durrës, Albania. Albanian native, English C1.
